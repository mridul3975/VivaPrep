## Recording boundaries

Fresh footage of the current local build, including the refined RV logo, direct guest/demo entry, subjective questions, editable and confirmed Prisma transcripts, evidence-linked Groq feedback, follow-up questions, uploaded-source debugging, browser tests, per-answer recorders, repair viva, History and usage limits.

The recording uses an isolated PostgreSQL database and an illustrative guest session. The spoken learner answer is prerecorded synthetic speech passed through the browser recorder and the real Prisma API. The repair defense is scripted example content. Groq generation and reviews and browser test execution are real. Processing waits are edited out and disclosed in the video.

The filmed Study answer scored 100%; the repair viva scored 75%. These are illustrative product results, not measurements of student learning. Python is not executed by the app; the recorded debugging tests execute JavaScript in the browser.

Timbre is used to produce this external voiceover, not presented as an in-app text-to-speech feature. The app uses Gnani Prisma v3.0 for transcription and Groq for question generation/review.

No production deployment, social posting or submission was performed. Older recordings and your original voiceover cut are preserved.
